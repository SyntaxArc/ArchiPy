# 5.12.1 — 2026-10-14

[← 5.12.0](5.12.0.md) | [↑ 5.x series](index.md)

## Fixed

### Adapters - Kafka

- **Consume span ends at the commit** - The Kafka instrumentor opens a `<topic> process` span when a message is polled
  and ended it only on the next `poll`, `consume` or `close`. The span then covered idle time and stayed the current span
  of the thread, so unrelated spans became its children. `KafkaConsumerAdapter.commit` now ends it.
    - `batch_consume` produces one span for the whole batch, with a link to every message; commit once after the batch
    - A consumer that never commits, such as one with `ENABLE_AUTO_COMMIT=true`, keeps the previous behaviour
    - The async consumer adapter is unchanged, because its polls run in an executor thread

## Tests

- **Kafka consume spans** - New scenarios cover a batch, one message at a time, consecutive polls without a commit and
  auto-commit, against the real Kafka container and the OpenTelemetry Collector.
- **Test isolation** - The Kafka span scenarios no longer leave an open span that made a later scenario record a span
  despite a sample ratio of zero. `ConfluentKafkaInstrumentor.uninstrument` does not unwrap `consume` or `close`, so
  binding it again wrapped `consume` twice and one batch produced two consume spans, the first never ended. The test
  cleanup now unwraps them. Production binds the instrumentor once and is not affected.

## Chore

- **Type checker** - `ty` 0.0.87 reports narrowed field overrides in the SQLite and StarRocks configs, so those two
  fields carry a documented `ty: ignore`.
- **Kafka tutorial** - Describes how consume spans end for batches, single messages and auto-commit.

## Dependencies

- **Lock file and lower bounds** - `uv.lock` is refreshed and the lower bounds follow it: `async-lru>=2.4.0` (Keycloak,
  MinIO and ScyllaDB packages), `boto3>=1.43.113` (MinIO package), and the development tools `boto3-stubs>=1.43.113`,
  `pre-commit>=4.7.0` and `ty>=0.0.87`. Transitive updates: `botocore` 1.43.113, `botocore-stubs` 1.43.113, `markdown`
  3.11.1, `sentry-sdk` 2.72.0, `starlette` 1.8.0, `truststore` 0.10.5, `types-s3transfer` 0.19.2 and `wrapt` 2.5.1.

# 6.1.1 — 2026-11-23

[← 6.1.2](6.1.2.md) | [6.1.0 →](6.1.0.md) | [↑ 6.x series](index.md)

## Chore

- **Type Checking** - Set 21 ty rules to `error`.
    - **Type Names** - Replaced `mismatched-type-name` with `mismatched-enum-name`, `mismatched-namedtuple-name`,
      `mismatched-newtype-name`, `mismatched-paramspec-name`, `mismatched-type-alias-name`, `mismatched-typeddict-name`,
      `mismatched-typevar-name`, and `mismatched-typevartuple-name`.
    - **Classes and Overrides** - Enabled `abstract-class-instantiation`, `incompatible-mutable-override`,
      `incompatible-property-override`, `invalid-dataclass-post-init`, and `lost-keyword-for-regular-parameter`.
    - **Narrowing and Returns** - Enabled `disjoint-type-is-narrowing`, `suspicious-type-is-narrowing`,
      `invalid-generator-return-type`, and `invalid-implicit-return`.
    - **Member and Key Checks** - Enabled `missing-module-member`, `missing-union-attribute`,
      `non-literal-typed-dict-key`, and `unknown-typed-dict-constructor-key`.

## Dependencies

- **ty** - Require `ty>=0.0.108` and lock version `0.0.108`.
